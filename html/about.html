Reflection on My Time at ECTS

My Growth at Erie County Technical School During my time at Erie County Technical School (ECTS), I have grown both personally and professionally. When I first started, I was still developing my programming and technology skills, but over time, I have become more confident in my ability to solve problems, learn new technologies, and build projects independently. ECTS has given me opportunities to gain hands-on experience and apply what I have learned to projects that reflect my interests and future career goals. One of the biggest areas of growth for me has been web development. Through working on my personal portfolio website, I have learned how to use HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to create and improve web pages. I have also worked on organizing my projects, adding my resume, and creating individual pages to showcase my work. While developing my website, I have faced challenges with website structure, styling, and deployment. Working through these problems has helped me become better at debugging code, researching solutions, and understanding how different parts of a website work together. Another project that has helped me grow is my React game API project. Working with React and an API has helped me understand how applications can retrieve and display information dynamically. This project has given me experience working with tools and concepts beyond basic HTML and CSS. It has also taught me that building an application requires planning, testing, and troubleshooting to make sure everything works as intended. I have also explored artificial intelligence by developing my own AI agent using Python and Ollama. Through this project, I have learned more about Python programming, connecting software to AI models, and building features such as file management, calculations, and memory. I have also worked on making the agent safer by adding file backups, workspace restrictions, and protections against editing certain files. This experience has helped me understand that programming is not just about making something work; it is also about making sure it works reliably and safely. These projects have taught me the importance of independence, patience, and continuous learning. When I encounter an error or something does not work as expected, I have learned to break the problem into smaller parts, research possible solutions, and test changes instead of giving up. I have also learned that technology is constantly evolving, which means I need to continue improving my skills and learning new tools throughout my career. As I prepare for graduation, I feel more confident about pursuing a future in programming and technology. My experiences at Erie County Technical School have helped me build technical knowledge, develop problem-solving skills, and turn my ideas into real projects. I am proud of the progress I have made and the projects I have worked on. I know I still have more to learn, but I plan to use the foundation I have built at ECTS to continue growing, take on new challenges, and work toward my future career goals.
