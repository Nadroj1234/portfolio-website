Field Trip Reflections

A collection of my experiences visiting colleges and businesses through Erie County Technical School.

Penn State Behrend Field Trip

November 21, 2025

On our field trip to Penn State Behrend, we started the day by watching an exciting video about NASA’s new program to return humans to the Moon, which helped set the tone for an inspiring visit. After that, we explored the planetarium and learned more about space and astronomy. We also toured the campus, where we got to see the computer lab and the impressive esports lab.

One of the highlights of the trip was a hands-on activity where we programmed iRobots to draw on a whiteboard, letting us experiment with coding in a fun and creative way. Overall, the experience was both educational and memorable.

Erie Insurance Field Trip

April 29, 2025

On April 29, 2025, I went on a field trip to Erie Insurance. One place we visited on the tour was a mail room where they prepare and ship all of the mail they get. Another place I visited was the house room. This room had a real size house and everything like the siding, windows, and roofing were different materials. This house is used for new house inspectors and contractors to practice on. The last room I visited was a fitness center. It costs $15 to use the center.

It looked big and had a lot of machines and things you could use.

After the tour we went back to the main room and 6 presentators came in. There jobs were a cloud engineer, info SEC, IT system administrator, Senior IT business support specialists, professional business systems analyst, and a senior programmer. They all covered their story on how they started working at Erie Insurance. They talked about what they do in each of their professions.

After the presentations we ate lunch then did an activity. We had to split our team in half and have IT workers and stakeholders as the two teams. The IT workers had to ask the stakeholders what they wanted their program to look like. Our program was about a library book reservation system. Halfway through the activity the stakeholders got a change in what they wanted it to look like. In the end my team of IT workers was able to figure out what the stakeholders wanted.

I see Erie Insurance as a great place to work at. I will definitely consider working there. This tour overall really helped me learn the different fields that Eire Insurance has to offer.

Gannon University Field Trip

November 22, 2024

We went on a tour to Gannon University for a field trip. I really enjoyed the tour and all of the different buildings in Gannon. There is a lot of sports and activities that they offer at Gannon. I might apply for Gannon depending on whether I found a better University or not. The tuition to go to Gannon is around $50,000 or so depending on what lab you take and if you live on campus. We toured the two freshman dorms and the rec center with the indoor field and gym.

We got Little Cesar’s for lunch and it was good. At the end of the tour we did an activity called Sys Admin Lab on Windows. We also talked about Hacking/ Cyber Patiot. I appreciate Gannon for letting us come out and tour their campus today.
